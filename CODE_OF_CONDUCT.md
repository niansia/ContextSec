# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in ContextSec a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive, evidence-based feedback.
- Preserve reviewer disagreement instead of turning technical debate into personal conflict.
- Take responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Put the health of the project and its community ahead of individual preference.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring a reporter to disclose vulnerability details publicly.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through a repository issue with only the minimum necessary information. For concerns involving a vulnerability, sensitive personal information, or a credible safety risk, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting in the repository's **Security** tab. Do not place exploit details, secrets, or personal data in a public issue.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Enforcement decisions should protect privacy and explain the applicable community standard to the extent safely possible.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially representing ContextSec in public spaces.
